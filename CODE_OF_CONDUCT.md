# Code of Conduct

## The Short Version

Be respectful, stay on topic, and act in good faith. We're here to build great software together.

## Guidelines

- Be constructive in discussions, code reviews, and issue reports
- Respect that contributors have different experience levels and perspectives
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its technical goals
- No spam, harassment, or personal attacks

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that don't align with these guidelines. Disruptive participants may be banned at maintainer discretion.

## Contact

Report issues to security@workermill.com.
