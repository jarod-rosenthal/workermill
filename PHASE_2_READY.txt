EXECUTION FLOW - BEFORE VS AFTER

BEFORE (Sequential):
  Dispatch → S0(queued) → S1(blocked) → S2(blocked)
              ↓ (wait)
           S0 completes
              ↓ (unblock)
           S1(queued) → S1(running)
              ↓ (wait)
           S1 completes
              ↓ (unblock)
           S2(queued) → S2(running)

Total time: ~30 minutes (3 stories × 10 min + wait times)

AFTER (Parallel):
  Dispatch → S0(queued) → S0(running)
             S1(queued) → S1(running)  ← All simultaneously!
             S2(queued) → S2(running)

Total time: ~10 minutes (all 3 run in parallel)
