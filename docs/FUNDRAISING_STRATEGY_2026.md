## WorkerMill's Differentiation

### The Pitch in One Sentence

> "WorkerMill lets you design your own AI engineering team - custom personas for your stack, multi-provider routing, real-time coordination on Epics."

| Feature | Copilot | Cursor | Devin | WorkerMill |

| **Model** | Autocomplete | AI IDE | Single agent | Multi-agent orchestration |
| **Customization** | None | Limited | None | Full Persona Studio |
| **Multi-model** | No | No | No | Per-persona routing |
| **Visibility** | None | Some | Limited | Real-time coordination feed |
| **Autonomy** | Low | Medium | High | High + configurable |

### Technical Moats

1. **Persona Studio** - Customers create personas for THEIR stack (Unity, Shopify, Terraform, etc.)
2. **Provider Routing** - Route QA → Gemini, Backend → Claude, DevOps → Ollama
3. **Coordination Protocol** - Real-time decisions, questions, sibling context checks
4. **BYOK** - Zero markup on AI costs, full cost transparency

**Slide 3: Solution**

**Slide 7: Market Size**

**Slide 8: Traction**

## Fundraising Timeline

### Typical Seed Round: 11-15 Weeks

**Weeks 1-2: Preparation**
- Finalize pitch deck
- Build target investor list (50-100 names)
- Identify warm intro paths
- Prep data room (financials, metrics, legal docs)

**Weeks 3-5: Outreach Sprint**
- Send 20-30 emails per week
- Aim to schedule meetings in tight 2-3 week window
- Creates urgency and FOMO

**Weeks 6-10: Meetings & Follow-ups**
- First meetings (30 min intros)
- Partner meetings (deeper dives)
- Due diligence requests

**Weeks 11-15: Close**
- Term sheet negotiations
- Legal review
- Wire funds

### Buffer
Give yourself **6 months runway**:
- 3 months fundraising
- 3 months buffer if it takes longer
