## About Section

WorkerMill is an autonomous AI engineering team — not a copilot. Point it at your backlog and wake up to pull requests.

Give it a Jira ticket, GitHub Issue, or a plain-English spec. WorkerMill decomposes the work into stories, assigns expert personas (backend, frontend, DevOps, QA, security), executes in parallel, runs your CI/CD pipeline, and delivers a tech-lead-reviewed PR ready for your approval.

How it works:
- Planning agent decomposes complex work into executable stories
- 12 built-in expert personas + custom persona creation
- Mandatory tech lead review on every PR (up to 3 revision cycles)
- Real-time dashboard with live terminal logs, cost tracking, and blocker escalation

Local-first by design. The WorkerMill agent runs on your machine. Your code is cloned locally, executed locally, and pushed from your machine. We never touch your source code.

Works with what you already use: Jira, Linear, GitHub Issues. GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket repos. Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, or Ollama models. Bring your own keys — zero AI markup.

Free forever on your own hardware. Pro at $14.50/mo for parallel workers, cloud execution, and team features.

Bootstrapped and founder-led. Built for engineers, by an engineer.
